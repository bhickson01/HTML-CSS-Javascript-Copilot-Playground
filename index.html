Sweet & Spicy Salmon Bowls with Garlic Spinach and Jasmine Rice

Craving something quick, healthy, and full of flavor? This Sweet & Spicy Salmon Bowl brings together tender glazed salmon, garlicky sautéed spinach, and fragrant ginger jasmine rice for a satisfying, vibrant meal. Perfect for weeknights or meal prep, this bowl is easy to make and customizable with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • Salmon: 2 salmon fillets, 2 tbsp honey/maple syrup, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sriracha, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 garlic clove (minced), salt & pepper
  • Rice: 1 cup jasmine rice, 2 cups water, 1 tsp grated fresh ginger, pinch of salt
  • Spinach: 2 cups fresh spinach, 1 tsp olive or sesame oil, 1 garlic clove (sliced), red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Toppings: Green onions, sesame seeds, lime wedges, avocado

Instructions

  1. Cook Rice: Rinse rice. In a pot, combine rice, water, ginger, and salt. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 15 min. Let rest 5 min.
  2. Cook Salmon: Mix sauce ingredients. Sear salmon for ~4 min per side. Pour sauce over and cook 1–2 min to glaze.
  3. Sauté Spinach: Heat oil, cook garlic for 30 sec. Add spinach and sauté until wilted. Add chili flakes if desired.
  4. Build Bowl: Add rice, top with spinach and salmon. Finish with toppings of choice.
